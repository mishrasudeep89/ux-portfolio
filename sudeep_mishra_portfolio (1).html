Fintech · P2P Payments

Reducing friction in peer-to-peer payment flows

Problem: Cluttered UI with redundant QR options and ambiguous Send vs Receive actions caused cognitive overload and incomplete transactions.
Before
  • Multiple QR entry points
  • Mixed Send / Receive
  • Extra unnecessary steps
  • Unclear CTA hierarchy
After
  • Single unified QR point
  • Clear action separation
  • Streamlined flow steps
  • Progressive disclosure
📈 ~20–30% estimated improvement in transaction completion speed and usability scores; significant reduction in user confusion.

Food Delivery · Checkout UX

Rebuilding trust through checkout transparency

Problem: Hidden pricing, COD auto-booking, intrusive popups, and no cancellation window caused drop-offs and eroded user trust at checkout.
Before
  • Hidden pricing details
  • COD auto-booked
  • No cancellation option
  • Intrusive popups
After
  • Transparent pricing
  • Explicit confirmation step
  • Cancellation window
  • Non-intrusive feedback
📉 ~15–25% estimated reduction in checkout drop-offs; improved user trust scores and smoother end-to-end experience.
